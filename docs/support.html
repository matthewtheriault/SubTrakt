SubTrakt Support

Get help

To report a bug or ask a question, open an issue on GitHub.

Common questions

I'm not getting reminders. Open the iOS Settings app, go to Notifications → SubTrakt, and turn on Allow Notifications. Reminders go off at 9:00 AM, as many days before each charge or trial end as you've set in SubTrakt's Settings.

Where is my data stored? Only on your device. There is no account or cloud sync. See the privacy policy.

How do I move my data to a new phone? Restore the new phone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one, and your SubTrakt data comes with it.
