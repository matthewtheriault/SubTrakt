DueDate Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

DueDate is a subscription tracker that runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, or share any of your data.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter in the app stays on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. That includes subscription names, amounts, dates, notes, any logo images you choose, and your settings. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server.

What we collect

Nothing. DueDate contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking software, and it makes no network requests.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, DueDate schedules reminders on your device using the system's local notifications. They are created and delivered on your device and never pass through a server.

Photos

If you pick an image to use as a subscription's logo, the system photo picker gives DueDate only the image you select. DueDate stores that image on your device and cannot see the rest of your photo library.

Backups

Your data may be included in your device's iCloud or computer backups, which Apple's own privacy terms cover.

Deleting your data

You can delete any subscription in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

DueDate collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version on this page and change the effective date above.
